About

Where clear thinking meets AI

Prompt engineering is the skill of guiding an AI model with clear, structured instructions.

What you will do

You receive a story, scene instructions and reference images at the start. Your task is to generate an original AI video that follows them, using your own laptop and AI tools, and refine your prompts until the result matches.

Skills you can show

  • Structured, well-refined prompting
  • Accuracy to the given scenario
  • Visual quality and consistency
  • Creativity under a strict time limit

Rules

Rules and guidelines

Read these carefully before you register.

Participation and tools

  • Individual entry or a team of maximum two members.
  • Exactly 120 minutes, including generation, editing and submission.
  • Students may bring and use their own laptops.
  • Free, Pro and Premium AI tools are allowed (e.g., Runway, Kling, Pika, Sora, CapCut, Flow).
  • AI assistants (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) and editing software are allowed for prompt support.
  • Participants arrange their own tool accounts, credits and subscriptions.

Video and prompt requirements

  • Follow the provided story, scene instructions and required visual elements; use the supplied images appropriately.
  • Use clear, structured prompts and refine them to improve motion, continuity and visual consistency.
  • Create the video during the competition; previously completed videos cannot be submitted.
  • Submit a playable MP4. Specific length, aspect ratio and conditions will be stated in the task.
  • Editing, audio and subtitles are allowed, but AI generation must form the core video content.

Fairness and conduct

  • Do not copy another team’s prompts or work, fabricate generation evidence, or include unauthorized or harmful content.
  • Judges may inspect prompts and verify the work. Their evaluation decision is final.
